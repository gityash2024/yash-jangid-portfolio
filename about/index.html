About

Curious mind.
Builder by nature.

I’m Yash Jangid, a Senior Full Stack Engineer based in Gurugram. I like problems where the product spans multiple layers: complex interfaces, APIs, real-time data, infrastructure, and increasingly AI-driven workflows.

Currently at Imaging IQ

I care about systems that are useful after the demo.

My work has moved through e-commerce, Web3, real-time trading systems, job-tech and now healthcare AI. The domains changed, but the pattern stayed consistent: understand the real workflow, reduce accidental complexity, and build software that remains maintainable when users and edge cases arrive.

Today, I’m building medical-imaging and AI workflows involving DICOM/NIfTI ingestion, OHIF, Orthanc, orchestration services, LLM workflows and backend systems. Before that, I helped build and scale multiple Web3 and trading products, improved platform latency, reduced deployment times, and created reusable packages for engineering teams.

What I optimize for

Clarity. Good architecture should make the next decision easier, not harder.

Ownership. I like seeing a problem through—from understanding it to shipping, debugging and improving it in production.

Measured impact. Performance, reliability and delivery speed matter most when they change the experience for real users and teams.

Outside the ticket

I spend a lot of time exploring new AI engineering workflows, developer tools and architecture patterns. I’m especially interested in agentic development, MCP, LLM orchestration and how these tools change the way small teams can build ambitious products.
