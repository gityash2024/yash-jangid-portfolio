From product interfaces to platform ownership

I started with frontend delivery, API integration, debugging and production features. That quickly expanded into backend systems, database design, WebSockets, microservices, CI/CD and platform architecture.

At ITH Technologies, I worked across Web3, trading, job-tech and enterprise products. The work included architecture for 5+ Web3 platforms, systems serving 10,000+ users, high-throughput realtime APIs and reusable internal packages.

Now: healthcare AI and medical imaging

At Imaging IQ, I’m building workflows around DICOM/NIfTI ingestion, OHIF Viewer, Orthanc, backend orchestration and AI-assisted analysis. The interesting part is not any single technology — it’s making many systems behave like one coherent product.

Why agentic development interests me

I’m especially interested in LLM orchestration, MCP, structured tool execution and AI-assisted engineering workflows. The goal is not novelty; it’s building systems that increase capability without reducing observability or control.

Engineering principles

How I make technical decisions.

These are the defaults I return to when the brief is unclear, the system is complex, or the deadline is real.

01 / CLARITY

Architecture should reduce decisions.

Good boundaries make future work easier instead of creating abstraction for its own sake.

02 / PERFORMANCE

Latency is part of product quality.

Fast systems feel trustworthy. I treat hot paths and user-visible delay as product concerns.

03 / AI

Orchestration over magic.

Models are one component. Reliable AI products need state, tools, validation, fallbacks and observability.

04 / DELIVERY

Shipping is an engineering feature.

CI/CD, testing and release reliability directly affect how quickly a team can learn and improve.
