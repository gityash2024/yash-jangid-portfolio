The challenge

Many systems. One clinical workflow.

Medical imaging software is not just a viewer. Data must be ingested, categorized, processed, visualized and coordinated across services while preserving traceability and a usable workflow for clinical teams.

The engineering challenge is building a reliable platform layer around those moving parts without hiding the state of the pipeline or coupling every component together.

What I’m building

From ingestion to AI-assisted analysis.

  • DICOM/NIfTI ingestion, processing and pipeline-management workflows.
  • OHIF Viewer and Orthanc integration for radiology workflows.
  • Backend APIs and orchestration layers for structured processing.
  • LLM/AI workflow execution and AI-assisted imaging analysis.
  • Scalable UI workflows connecting platform state to user actions.
Engineering lens

Make complex state legible.

For this kind of system, the product quality depends heavily on visibility: what arrived, what is running, what failed, what output was produced, and what the user can do next. I treat orchestration state, observability and interface design as one connected problem.
